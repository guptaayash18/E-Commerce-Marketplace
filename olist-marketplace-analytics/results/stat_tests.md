# Hypothesis tests

## 1. 1-star review rate: late vs on-time delivered orders

- late: 3,539 of 6,534 = 54.2% (95% CI 53.0%–55.4%)
- on time: 6,160 of 89,936 = 6.8% (95% CI 6.7%–7.0%)
- risk ratio 7.9x; chi-square = 15,079, dof = 1, p < 0.001

## 2. Repeat-purchase rate: late vs on-time first order

- first order late: 160 of 6,355 = 2.52% (95% CI 2.16%–2.93%)
- first order on time: 2,641 of 86,995 = 3.04% (95% CI 2.92%–3.15%)
- difference 0.52 pp (95% CI 0.12 to 0.92 pp); z = 2.34, p 0.019

Both effects are statistically significant at 96k orders; only the first is large enough to matter commercially.
